Hello! I'm Sosina Alofipo, a lifelong learner and aspiring instructional designer. I have a deep interest in educational technology and its potential to transform learning experiences. My background spans across curriculum development, instructional design, and digital learning solutions.

With roots in Pacific Islander culture, I bring a unique perspective to my work in education. I believe in creating inclusive and engaging learning environments that celebrate diversity and promote student success.

When I'm not working on educational projects, you can find me exploring new music, looking for new places to eat, or enjoying time with my family.

Information Literacy in the Age of AI

This multimedia project explores the importance of information literacy in the age of AI. It provides a comprehensive overview of the key concepts and skills needed to navigate today's digital landscape effectively.

BSG Tips For U

This interactive design project is a collection of tips and tricks for first-year business students. It includes a variety of helpful links and firsthand advice from the Business Student Government members.

This interactive design project is a collection of resources for navigating the David Eccles School of Business, mainly to introduce first-year students to the many resources offered to first-year students.

Canvas LMS Course: Eccles Beginnings: Navigating U

A summer transition course for incoming University of Utah business students, designed to foster confidence, engagement, and readiness before the first semester begins. The course blends interactive learning activities with practical resources to help students navigate academic expectations and campus life.

Canvas LMS Course: Finding Balance: Stress Management Strategies for First-Year Graduate Students

A self-paced course designed to help first-year graduate students identify personal stressors, explore evidence-based coping strategies, and create a customized stress management plan. The course guides students through three key areas—Thrive (physical and mental health), Succeed (academic and career balance), and Matter (purpose, community, and social connection)—to support both academic success and overall well-being.
